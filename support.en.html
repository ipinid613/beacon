Beacon Support

Beacon - Lights & Siren is an iPhone app for iOS 27 or later. Use flashing lights, a siren and optional video recording without an account or internet connection. The app interface is currently in Korean.

Start and stop

  1. Swipe left or right on the main screen to choose a preset. Open Settings to adjust the lights, flashing, siren, brightness and recording, then save.
  2. Check the selected audio output and media volume before tapping START. If an external output is selected, the app suggests switching to the iPhone speaker and asks whether to continue.
  3. While recording, use the camera button in the preview to switch between the front and rear cameras. Each new recording starts with the front camera.
  4. Tap the stop icon at the bottom right to reveal the large STOP button. Tap STOP to end the effects and recording and return to the main screen. Tapping outside STOP returns to the small stop icon.
  5. A confirmation appears on the main screen after the video has actually been saved to Photos. Preview in Settings only tests the alert effects; it does not record.

Sound and audio recording

The siren follows the system's selected audio output and media volume. Adjust the volume using the main screen slider or iPhone Control Centre. The siren plays even in silent mode. An audio output change or interruption, such as a phone call, ends the effects. They do not restart automatically.

The microphone records the siren along with surrounding sounds. Beacon does not apply echo cancellation or noise suppression, so a loud siren may mask speech. For clearer conversations, lower the media volume or turn off the siren in the preset before starting. Disconnect headphones and begin at a low volume when testing the sound.

Permissions and saving

Recording requires Camera and Microphone permissions. Saving requires permission to add to Photos. Check these permissions for Beacon in iPhone Settings. Recording works without location permission; the video then indicates that location is unavailable. The time is UTC from the device clock. Coordinates include available accuracy and labels for approximate or stale locations.

When the app moves to the background or is interrupted by locking or a call, recording ends and the app attempts to save. If permission or a save failure prevents adding the video to Photos, the original remains in the app and saving is retried on a later launch or activation. Check available storage and Photos permission, then reopen Beacon. Deleting Beacon before saving completes can remove the local original. Recovery or saving after a force quit, crash or power loss is not guaranteed.

Data and deletion

Video, audio, location and presets are processed on your device and are not automatically sent to a developer server. Delete saved videos in Photos and user presets in Beacon Settings. For deleting all internal app data and revoking permissions, see the Privacy Policy.
