Beacon Privacy Policy

App: Beacon - Lights & Siren
Last updated: 12 October 2026
Developer: Yeongjin Jeon (전영진)

Information processed by the app

Beacon works on your device without registration. The developer does not collect your videos, audio, locations, presets or usage records on a server through the app. Beacon has no advertising, tracking, third-party analytics SDKs or automatic video uploads.

InformationPurpose and processing
Camera video and microphone audioWhen recording is enabled, Beacon records the scene and creates a video file on your device. You can switch between front and rear cameras during recording. It does not recognise faces or extract biometric information to identify people.
Recording time and locationBeacon shows the device clock in UTC and embeds permitted coordinates and horizontal accuracy in video frames. It labels unavailable, approximate and stale locations. Time is not verified against an external time server.
Presets and selectionNames, light settings, flashing, siren, brightness, recording settings and the last selection are stored locally within the app.

Permissions and choices

Beacon requests Camera and Microphone permissions for recording and permission to add videos to Photos for saving. It does not request permission to read your existing photo library. Location access is requested only while using the app. You can deny it and still record video and audio without location.

If Camera or Microphone permission is unavailable, recording does not start, but you can still use the alert effects. Turn recording off in the preset or change and revoke individual permissions in iPhone Settings. Beacon ends recording when it moves to the background; it does not continue filming in the background.

Retention and deletion

Videos are first recorded to local app storage. Once adding a video to Photos is confirmed, Beacon deletes its temporary original. If Photos permission is denied or saving fails, the original remains in the app and saving is retried on a later launch or activation. Unreadable, incomplete files may not be recoverable. Automatic recovery or saving is not guaranteed. Videos awaiting saving have no fixed automatic deletion deadline.

Delete videos saved to Photos using the Photos app. To remove them completely, also check Recently Deleted. To delete all unsaved videos and presets inside Beacon, delete the app itself. Offloading may retain app data. Videos already saved to Photos remain after deleting Beacon. See Apple's storage guide and Apple's guide to deleting photos and videos for device management instructions.

The folder for videos awaiting saving is excluded from app data backup. Photos syncing and backup, and other device backups, may occur separately according to your iCloud and iPhone settings. Beacon does not delete copies already stored in Photos or iCloud on your behalf.

External services and support enquiries

Beacon does not upload videos to a developer server or automatically send them by email, message or to your contacts. If you share a video yourself through Photos, that service's policies apply. Apple's Photos, iCloud, TestFlight and device diagnostics services are governed by your Apple service settings and policies.

If you send a support enquiry, your email address and enquiry contents are provided to the developer to respond. The developer reviews and retains them to the extent and for the period needed to answer the enquiry and resolve the issue. You may request deletion using the contact below.

Privacy enquiries and deletion requests: ipinid613@kakao.com. The developer cannot remotely access or delete videos or presets stored only on your device.

Policy changes

If the app's information handling changes, this page and its effective date will be updated.
